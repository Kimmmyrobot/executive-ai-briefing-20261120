Questions

Executive Ai Briefing questions for working teams

How is an executive briefing different from a keynote?

A briefing is more confidential, decision-focused and interactive, with content shaped around one leadership team's operating context.

Who should attend?

Include leaders responsible for the business outcome, technology, data, security, people, finance and any function directly affected by the decision.

What should the briefing produce?

It should produce a common vocabulary, explicit assumptions, priority questions, decision owners and a practical next step.
